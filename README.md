

MediSum — Smart Medical Document Synthesis
Project Overview
MediSum is an AI-powered medical document synthesis platform designed to transform complex, lengthy, and fragmented medical records into clear, concise, and actionable summaries. It bridges the gap between raw clinical documentation and meaningful insights for healthcare professionals, patients, and researchers.

Problem Statement
Healthcare generates enormous volumes of documents — discharge summaries, lab reports, prescriptions, diagnostic notes, and imaging reports. Clinicians spend a significant portion of their time reading and interpreting these documents rather than focusing on patient care. MediSum solves this by automating intelligent synthesis of medical content.

Key Features

Multi-document Synthesis — Aggregates information from multiple medical records (PDFs, scans, reports) into a unified summary
Clinical NLP Engine — Understands medical terminology, abbreviations, and context using domain-specific language models
Patient-friendly Summaries — Generates easy-to-read versions of complex medical documents for patients
Doctor-ready Briefs — Produces structured clinical briefs highlighting key diagnoses, medications, allergies, and history
Temporal Tracking — Identifies and organizes medical events chronologically for longitudinal patient history
Red Flag Detection — Flags critical findings, abnormal values, or urgent conditions needing immediate attention

Data Intelligence

Structured Export — Outputs JSON/XML/FHIR-compliant data for interoperability with EHR systems.
Analytics Dashboard — Provides statistics on medication usage, frequency of diagnoses, and patient trends.
Confidence Scoring — Assigns reliability scores to extracted information based on OCR/NLP certainty.
Audit Trail — Logs all analysis steps for compliance and medico-legal accountability.

User Experience

Dual-View Summaries — Patient-friendly narrative + clinician-ready structured brief side by side.
Interactive Timeline — Clickable events for medications, tests, and diagnoses across time.
Urgency Prioritization — Sorts findings by severity (critical → moderate → routine).
Mobile-Friendly UI — Optimized for tablets/phones used in clinics.
